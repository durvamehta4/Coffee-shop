# Changelog — 100 bold iterations

Each entry is one shipped, user-facing change aimed at conversions or look & feel.

1. **Order-ahead pickup** — every menu item gets an "Add" button; a slide-in cart drawer with quantity controls, a pickup-time picker (15-min slots within today's hours) and pay-at-pickup confirmation. Menu is now data-driven (`js/data.js`). Mobile header gets an always-visible cart icon.
2. **Drink builder** — tapping "Add" on a drink opens a customization sheet (size, hot/iced, milk, extra shots, sweetness) with live pricing and an animated SVG cup whose layers change with every choice. Bottom-sheet on mobile.
3. **Hero quick-order card** — a frosted-glass card in the hero with a live "Open · closes in Xh" status (pulsing dot), ready-time estimate and one-tap add for the three most popular drinks. Also hides the closed drawer so its shadow no longer bleeds onto the page.
4. **Pastry pairing upsell** — when the cart has more drinks than pastries, the drawer suggests a croissant or muffin at "$1 off with every drink", applied automatically as a discount line. Adds a pluggable discount-rules system (`Cart.rules`).
5. **Visual menu cards** — the text list becomes a card grid; each drink shows an SVG illustration drawn from its real recipe (same renderer as the builder), food gets a big illustrated badge. Hover lift + tilt; compact 2-column grid on phones.
6. **Floating order bar** — once the cart has items, a pinned "N items · Checkout → $total" bar appears (full-width on phones, pill on desktop), bouncing on every add and hiding while the drawer is open.
7. **"Find your cup" quiz** — dark feature section with a 3-question tap quiz (taste, hot/iced, moment) that recommends a drink, renders it in the house cup style, and opens the builder pre-set to hot/iced. Builder now accepts presets.
8. **Ember Club loyalty card** — tilted, gradient stamp card with 9 stamps + FREE; join with name/email for a welcome stamp, earn a stamp per drink on each order, and the 10th drink is automatically discounted (up to $6). Cart shows "You will earn N stamps" or a join nudge.
9. **"Evening roast" dark theme** — the whole palette moves to semantic design tokens; a full dark theme follows the system setting, with a header sun/moon toggle that is remembered (applied before paint, no flash). Cup illustrations get theme-aware outlines.
10. **Menu search & dietary filters** — search box plus toggle chips (Vegan, Under $5, Iced-friendly, Caffeine-free) that swap the tabs for a cross-category results grid with a live count, empty state and "Clear all". Also fixes self-referencing thumbnail tokens from iteration 9.
11. **Review wall** — rating summary plus two counter-scrolling marquee rows of review cards (pause on hover, static grid for reduced-motion users). Reviews are flagged as placeholders in the README.
12. **Motion & wayfinding** — staggered scroll-reveal for headings and cards, a header that is transparent over the hero and turns solid on scroll, a caramel scroll-progress line, and an animated underline on the nav link for the section in view. Reduced-motion users skip the reveals.
13. **Live order tracker** — after checkout a persistent corner card (survives reloads) shows Received → Brewing → Ready with a countdown ring timed to the pickup slot; it pulses while brewing, turns green when ready, and auto-expires 30 minutes after pickup.
14. **"Your usual?" one-tap reorder** — returning customers see their last order in a caramel banner at the top of the hero card ("Welcome back, Sam") with a Reorder button that rebuilds the exact customized cart; the empty cart offers the same shortcut.
15. **Retail bean shop + subscriptions** — "Take the roastery home" section with three illustrated SVG coffee bags (origin, tasting notes, 5-step roast meter), a grind picker and one-time vs. subscribe-and-save-15% plans; bags go into the same pickup cart. Swipeable on mobile. Fixes menu renderer clobbering same-id sections.
16. **Illustrated visit section** — a hand-drawn SVG neighborhood map (streets, park, river, drop-in pulsing pin, dark-theme variant) with Get directions / Call buttons, landmark-based address, amenity chips, and a full-week hours list that highlights today.
17. **After-hours pre-ordering** — the pickup picker now offers grouped Today / Tomorrow (pre-order) slots, so a closed shop still takes orders, with a friendly note when only tomorrow is available; the hero says "Closed now · pre-order for tomorrow 7 am" and the tracker shows hours for long waits.
18. **Gift card designer** — choose one of four designs, $15/$25/$50 or a custom amount, recipient, sender and message, with a live preview card that tilts in 3D and follows the pointer with a glare; adds to the pickup cart (handwritten card). New "Gifts" nav link.
19. **Café ambience mode** — a "Play café sounds" toggle in the hero synthesizes live coffeehouse atmosphere with Web Audio (swelling murmur from filtered brown noise, randomly panned cup clinks, occasional milk-steamer hiss); no audio files, off by default, with an animated equalizer while playing.
20. **Lean nav + rich footer** — the header is trimmed to the revenue paths (Menu, Beans, Rewards, Gifts, Visit + Order); About and Contact move to a new four-column footer with brand blurb, social links, Order/Company site maps, address, live "Today: 7 am – 6 pm" hours and back-to-top.
21. **Search & share presence** — `CafeOrCoffeeShop` JSON-LD (address, hours, price range, pickup order action) for rich results; Open Graph/Twitter tags with a branded 1200×630 share image rendered from the site's own cup illustration; SVG favicon; installable web manifest.
22. **Installable PWA** — service worker (network-first pages, stale-while-revalidate assets and fonts) for instant repeat visits and offline browsing; right after a first order, an "Order in two taps next time" card triggers the native Add-to-home-screen prompt (dismissal remembered).
23. **Time-aware happy hour** — weekday 2–4 pm "Afternoon chill" with 20% off iced drinks, auto-applied in the cart; a caramel announcement strip above the nav shows a live countdown before/during the deal and falls back to the pairing offer otherwise (dismissable per session).
24. **Daypart-aware hero** — eyebrow, headline, copy, card title and featured drinks change for morning ("Good coffee, slow mornings"), afternoon ("Beat the 3 pm slump", iced picks with struck-through happy-hour prices that open the builder pre-set to iced) and evening ("Wind down gently"); the hero photo gets a cool or dusk tint to match.
25. **Add-to-cart feedback** — each add launches a caramel dot that arcs into the cart (which pulses on arrival) and shows a toast ("✓ Iced Latte added (Large, Iced)") with View order and, for drinks without a pastry, a one-tap "+ 🥐 Croissant $1 off" add-on. Also fixes a mobile horizontal overflow caused by gift-form fieldsets.
26. **Shareable group orders** — "Ordering for a group? Share this order" in the cart encodes the cart into a link (native share sheet or clipboard); opening it shows "Sam shared an order (3 items)" with one-tap merge, so a team can pass one order around and add their drinks without accounts or a backend.
27. **Limited-time winter menu** — three seasonal drinks with their own cup recipes (gingerbread, peppermint mocha, maple oat cortado) in a frosty midnight-blue section with glassy cards, live "ends in N days" urgency and a canvas snowfall that only runs while visible (off for reduced motion); new "Winter ❄" menu tab.
28. **Holiday-aware hours engine** — one `hoursFor(date)` source of truth with holiday exceptions (Thanksgiving computed as the 4th Thursday, Christmas Eve/Day, New Year's, July 4) used by the open badge, hero card, footer, pickup slots (pre-orders skip closed days) and a rolling "next 7 days" hours list with holiday tags and an upcoming-closure notice.
29. **Keyboard & screen-reader pass** — "Skip to the menu" link, a consistent caramel focus ring, and proper modal behavior for the cart drawer and drink builder: dialog semantics, Tab/Shift-Tab focus trap, `inert` background while open, and focus returned to the control that opened them.
30. **Catering quote builder** — the generic contact form becomes a B2B tool: a headcount slider (5–150) and occasion chips auto-size a package (coffee boxes, pastry dozens, cold brew jugs, treat trays) with a live itemized estimate, free-delivery threshold and per-person cost; submitting opens a pre-filled quote email. A plain hello@ address stays for general contact.
31. **Weather-aware hero** — current conditions at the shop from Open-Meteo (no key, 4 s timeout, 30-min session cache) appear as a chip in the hero card ("☀️ 29°C in Brewtown · iced coffee weather", "perfect chai weather" when raining); hot days switch featured drinks to iced. Fails silently.
32. **Live caffeine & calorie meter** — the drink builder shows estimated caffeine (mg, with a bar against the 400 mg/day guideline that turns red past 60%) and calories, updating with size, milk, extra shots and sweetness. On mobile the "Add to order" button is now sticky at the bottom of the sheet.
33. **Merchandising badges** — menu cards get corner ribbons (★ Bestseller, Barista pick, New, ❄ Seasonal) and small dietary icons (🌱 vegan, 🌙 caffeine-free) to guide choice toward favorites.
34. **Pay-it-forward add-on** — a checkbox order bump in the cart ("☕ Pay a coffee forward · +$3") that adds a suspended coffee for the community board; warm brand moment and a natural AOV lift, with no fabricated counters.
35. **Editorial "Our story"** — emoji feature cards replaced by a magazine-style spread: oversized serif pull quote with caramel accent, story copy, and a numbered four-step farm-to-cup journey (Source → Roast → Rest → Brew) with custom SVG line icons on a dashed path. Scroll-reveal now covers the newer sections.
36. **⌘K quick-order palette** — press ⌘K / Ctrl+K / "/" (or tap the 🔍 header button) for a fuzzy-search palette over every menu item and site action (view order, reorder usual, dark mode, find us, quiz, gifts, catering). Arrow keys + Enter add items instantly or open the drink builder.
37. **Order by voice** — a 🎙 button in the hero and the ⌘K palette uses the Web Speech API; a clause-aware parser turns "two large iced oat lattes and a croissant" or "a double flat white with almond milk" into fully customized, correctly priced cart lines after a "Got it: …" confirmation. Hidden where speech recognition isn't supported.
38. **Richer pickup checkout** — pickup-mode tiles (Counter, Curbside with car description, Walk-up window), a "bring your own cup" toggle worth $0.25 off per drink, optional text-when-ready phone and barista note (tucked in a disclosure), the live total on the Place order button, and confirmation copy tailored to the chosen pickup mode. Toasts no longer peek over the open drawer.
39. **Celebratory confirmation + pickup QR** — placing an order fires a caramel/cream confetti burst from the drawer (skipped for reduced motion) and shows a scannable QR pass encoding the order number and name, generated by a lazily loaded cdnjs library (falls back to the order number alone).
40. **Performance pass: −28% LCP** — the time-of-day hero copy is applied by a tiny inline script before first paint (previously ~40 scripts had to load before the headline repainted), all scripts are `defer`red, and the image CDN is preconnected. Throttled-3G LCP went from ~1,130 ms to ~820 ms on both phone and desktop.
41. **Conversion analytics layer** — GA4-standard ecommerce events (`view_item_list`, `view_item`, `add_to_cart`, `view_cart`, `begin_checkout`, `purchase` with items, variants, discounts and pickup mode) plus quiz, voice, share and sign-up events pushed to `window.dataLayer`, ready for GTM/GA4. `?debug=funnel` shows a live step-by-step funnel overlay.
42. **Favorites + "My Ember" panel** — a ♡ button on every menu card saves favorites; a new 👤 header button opens an account-less "My Ember" panel with the stamp card, favorites (one-tap add) and the last 10 orders (one-tap reorder), all kept on the device.
43. **Mobile app-style tab bar** — on phones the crowded header (logo was wrapping onto two lines) is slimmed to logo + theme + menu, and a frosted bottom tab bar adds Home, Menu, Search, Me and a raised Order tab that shows the live badge and total (replacing the floating checkout bar there). Tracker, toasts and banners re-stack above it; menu tabs become one swipeable row.
44. **Interactive home brew guide** — choose Pour over / French press / AeroPress / Cold brew and 1–6 cups to get exact coffee grams, water, ratio, temperature and grind; a guided step timer with a progress ring (and haptic tap on step change) walks through bloom → pour → drawdown, and "Buy beans for this" jumps to the matching bag.
45. **Events & workshops** — a "What's on" section whose dates compute the next real occurrences (skipping holiday closures): a bookable Latte Art 101 workshop ($25, goes in the cart), a free Thursday cupping and Friday acoustic night; every event has a one-tap "Add to calendar" that downloads a valid `.ics` with a 1-hour reminder.
46. **"Ask the barista" smart FAQ** — type any question ("do you guys have oat milk?") or tap a suggestion and get an instant answer built from live data (holiday-aware hours, prices, menu) in a speech-bubble card with action buttons (filter vegan, add a cold brew, take the quiz, directions). A 10-question FAQ accordion ships with `FAQPage` JSON-LD for search rich results.
47. **Conversion-ordered page + chapter rail** — sections are re-sequenced by purchase intent (Hero → Winter specials → Menu → Quiz → Reviews → Rewards → Beans → Brew guide → Gifts → Events → Story → Visit → FAQ → Catering), and wide screens get a right-edge chapter rail of dots that labels itself on hover, highlights the current section and adapts to dark sections.
48. **Food detail sheets with pairings** — whole menu cards are now tap targets (drinks → builder, food → detail sheet). The sheet shows a large illustration, ingredients, allergen pills (gluten/dairy/egg), vegan and calorie tags, a quantity stepper with live total, and "Pairs well with" drinks rendered in the house cup style for one-tap cross-sell.
49. **Tactile visual polish** — a subtle SVG paper-grain overlay across the site (screen-blended in dark mode), organic wave edges cut into the dark quiz and winter sections with SVG masks, balanced heading line breaks (`text-wrap: balance/pretty`) and caramel text selection.
50. **Checkout safety net: E2E tests + CI** — a Playwright suite with a zero-dependency static server checks page errors and horizontal overflow at phone/desktop widths, the full builder → cart → checkout → purchase-event flow, pairing and happy-hour maths, the voice parser, Thanksgiving closures and shared-order links; GitHub Actions runs it on every push. README rewritten for the current architecture.
51. **First-order welcome offer** — a polite corner slide-in (after 25 s or on desktop exit intent) shown only to genuinely new visitors: enter an email to get 15% off the first order (capped at $10), applied automatically as a discount line and retired after that order; dismissal is permanent. Emits `view_promotion`/`select_promotion` events.
52. **Value-framed builder upsells** — Medium carries a "Most popular" anchor badge; when Small/Medium is selected a one-tap "Make it Large for +$0.75 · 33% more coffee" nudge appears (computed from real cup volumes), and a "Make it a combo" toggle adds a croissant at the pairing price, reflected live in the Add button total.
53. **Honest ETA, rush & last-call urgency** — the hero pill now shows the real earliest pickup ("ready by 8:30 AM") instead of a fixed "~10 min"; during typical morning/lunch rush windows a "🏃 Morning rush: order ahead and walk straight to the shelf" note appears; in the final 45 minutes the pill turns red with a live "Last call · order in the next 15 min for pickup today".
54. **Interactive flavor wheel** — an SVG wheel of eight taste slices (chocolate, nutty, caramel, fruity, floral, citrus, spice, berry) above the bean grid; picking a slice (click or keyboard) dims non-matching bags, lifts the matches and names a recommendation with a "See it →" jump.
55. **Friend referrals** — Ember Club members get an "Invite a friend" card with a personal `?ref=Name` link (native share or clipboard); invited visitors see a "Maya sent you a bonus stamp!" banner and start their card with two stamps instead of one, credited on the card itself.
56. **Forgiving, synced cart** — removing an item leaves a "Removed Blueberry Muffin · Undo" row for 6 seconds that restores it in place, and the cart syncs live across browser tabs (badge, drawer and order bar update instantly via the `storage` event).
57. **Print-perfect menu** — "🖨 Print menu" (or plain Ctrl/⌘+P) produces a clean one-page, two-column menu generated from live data (all categories, prices, vegan marks, hours, add-on pricing) with a scan-to-order QR code; the interactive page is hidden from print.
58. **Living hero** — the hero photo moves to its own layer with a slow 40-second Ken Burns drift and scroll parallax, and soft blurred SVG steam wisps rise and fade over the cups (screen-blended); all motion is disabled for reduced-motion users.
59. **Meet the baristas + signature picks** — four crew cards with illustrated SVG avatars, roles and fun facts; each has a signature order ("Iced oat vanilla latte", "Almond flat white, extra shot") and an "Order Maya's pick" button that opens the builder fully pre-configured (including combos). Swipeable on phones.
60. **Custom SVG icon system** — an inline sprite of consistent line icons (user, search, moon/sun, home, cup, bag) replaces OS-dependent emoji in the header buttons, theme toggle and mobile tab bar; icons inherit `currentColor` so they work in both themes.
61. **Re-engagement nudges** — switching tabs with items in the cart changes the title to "(2) ☕ Your order is waiting…" and draws a caramel count badge onto the favicon (restored on return); the order tracker offers "🔔 Notify me when it's ready", which fires a system notification at the pickup time.
62. **Holiday gift boxes** — three curated bundles (Morning Ritual, Origin Explorer, Cozy Nights) with illustrated SVG gift boxes that wiggle on hover, contents lists, struck-through value pricing and green "Save $9" ribbons; one-tap add to the pickup cart, swipeable on mobile.
63. **Pour-your-own latte art toy** — a canvas cup of speckled crema in the events section where you press and drag to pour milk: holding still blooms foam, faster strokes pull thinner lines (velocity-based), so you can wiggle a rosetta or pull a heart; save it as a PNG or book the real Latte Art 101 class.
64. **Plain-English coffee glossary** — jargon (ristretto, microfoam, cortado, single-origin, heirloom, washed, Swiss Water Process, ceremonial-grade, crema…) gets a dotted caramel underline in menu cards, the builder, detail sheets, beans and winter cards; hover, keyboard focus or tap shows a friendly one-line definition in a smart-positioned tooltip.
65. **Display & accessibility preferences** — an "Aa Display" footer button opens a panel with three text sizes, high-contrast mode (stronger text, borders and a deeper caramel in both themes), calm mode (stops every animation, snow, steam, marquee, confetti and parallax even without the OS setting) and underlined links; choices persist and apply before first paint.
66. **Subscription manager** — subscribe-and-save bags become managed subscriptions in My Ember: cadence pill, next pickup date and price, inline grind change, Skip next, Pause/Resume and Cancel; when a bag is due within a week, a "🫘 Your Ember House Blend is ready Thu, Dec 3" chip appears in the hero card.
67. **Living numbers** — the cart total, floating order bar, mobile Order tab and builder price roll smoothly (eased count-up) to each new value with a brief caramel flash, so upgrades and discounts visibly register; skipped for hidden elements, reduced-motion and calm mode, and always lands on the exact final text.
68. **Español** — an ES/EN header toggle translates the interface via a phrase dictionary applied to static and dynamically rendered text (nav, hero headlines for every daypart, menu tools and descriptions, drink builder, cart, checkout, section headings), with a `t()` helper for composed sentences; sets `<html lang>`, persists, and reverts cleanly to English. Product names stay as on the shop menu.
69. **Arrival-timed ordering** — "📍 I'm on my way" in checkout geolocates once (on-device only, never stored), computes haversine distance to the shop with walk/bike/drive estimates ("1.2 mi · 🚶 24 · 🚲 7 · 🚗 6 min") and auto-selects the pickup slot closest to the likely arrival time so drinks are fresh on arrival.
