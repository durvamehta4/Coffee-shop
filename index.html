    Our story

    Ember & Bean started in 2018 with a single 5-kilo roaster in a garage on Roast Street. We buy directly from a handful of small farms, roast in small batches every week, and brew every cup to order.

    Six years later the roaster is bigger, but the idea hasn't changed. Need a quick espresso on the way to work, or a quiet table for the afternoon? We're glad you're here.

    1. Source

      Direct trade with six farms, with fair prices paid straight to growers.

    2. Roast

      Small batches every Tuesday, profiled to bring out each origin.

    3. Rest

      Beans rest 3 to 7 days to de-gas, never more than a week from roast.

    4. Brew

      Dialed in every morning, poured to order, served the way you like it.
