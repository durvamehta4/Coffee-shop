Our story

Ember & Bean started in 2018 with a single roaster and a simple idea: coffee should taste like where it came from. We source directly from small farms, roast in small batches every week, and brew every cup to order.

Whether you need a quick espresso on the way to work or a quiet table for the afternoon, we're glad you're here.
